Scenario 02: Retry Storm

A slow service. Three clients. Follow how retries change the queue.

Mission briefing

The Retry Queue

  • DifficultyBeginner
  • Time3-5 minutes

You are investigating a service that is falling behind.

Three requests arrive just as the service slows down. Clients that do not get a response retry, but their original attempts are still waiting. How much work will remain in the queue?

Incident snapshot

Learning objectives

  • ✓Timeouts do not cancel queued work
  • ✓Retries add attempts to the queue
  • ✓Service capacity limits progress
  • ✓Bounded example, not a universal rule

Before you begin

This fixed teaching model uses a FIFO queue and at most two retries per client. Other systems may cancel, deduplicate, back off, or handle retries differently.

      Why?

      The slowdown limited the service to one attempt per round. B and C timed out while their original attempts remained queued, so their retries added work instead of replacing it. The original C attempt completed only after C had already sent two retries.

      Key lesson

      A timeout does not cancel the original work; a retry can add load to a queue that's already falling behind.

      This outcome follows only from this exercise's fixed capacity, FIFO order, timeout rule, and retry limit. Other systems may behave differently.
